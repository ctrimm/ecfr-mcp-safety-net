# Eval results, 2026-10-27/28: local LLM best tier on the silver set, a smaller Gemma, and thinking on

Follow-up to `golden/results-2026-10-27.md`. Shipped `data/` (bge-small), shipped code from PR #6
(`local-llm-lmstudio`), measured through `golden/eval_server.py --tier best` with
`ECFR_MCP_LLM_URL=http://localhost:1234/v1` and no `OPENROUTER_API_KEY`.

- Machine: Mac Studio M3 Ultra, 256 GB. LM Studio, models loaded with `--parallel 1`.
- `google/gemma-4-12b` is the 4-bit MLX build (6.77 GB); `google/gemma-4-e4b` is the default LM Studio build.
- Some runs shared the GPU with another run, so per-query times are upper bounds.

## Reading it

- **12B with thinking off matches Luna on silver**, overall and by type. The golden-set paraphrase
  weakness (0.58, n=12) does not hold at n=118 (0.68 vs Luna's 0.69).
- **e4b is too small.** It finds the right section (silver r@5 0.84) but ranks it poorly: r@1 0.58,
  paraphrase 0.42. On golden it is 0.04 above the free tier at r@1 (0.64 vs 0.60).
- **Thinking on is worse and ~9× slower.** Golden r@1 0.74 vs 0.84. Six of 45 LLM queries degraded;
  even if all six had been r@1 hits the ceiling is 0.86. Paraphrase r@1 fell to 0.42.

## Thinking on: reasoning that never ends

With thinking on, some rerank calls spend the whole `max_tokens` budget reasoning and return no JSON.
The batch retries once; if the retry also runs out, the query degrades to the free tier. From the
LM Studio log:

| Budget | Calls that hit the cap | Longest call that finished | LLM queries degraded |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16,384 | 6 of 54 (11%) | 3,967 | 1 of 7 (partial run) |
| 4,096 (the shipped value) | 13 of 118 (11%) | 3,806 | 4 of 19 (partial run) |
| 8,192 | 112 of 981 (11%) | 7,409 | 6 of 45 golden; 32 of 119 silver (stopped) |

- The cap-hit rate is about 11% at every budget, so most cap hits are runaways that no budget fixes.
- Most finished calls use 700–4,000 tokens, but the tail is long (7,409 at 8k), so a smaller budget
  also truncates real answers: golden q4 finished at 16k and 8k and degraded at 4k.
- Runaways are not tied to the query. Silver q3 finished at 4k and degraded at 8k, at temperature 0.
- LM Studio accepts `reasoning_effort` values none/minimal/low/medium/high/xhigh, but Gemma 4 only
  supports on/off: low, medium and high produce identical output (689 reasoning tokens on the same probe).
  `on` is rejected with HTTP 400.

The thinking-on silver run was stopped at 119/236 with 32 degraded (27%); with the golden result
already worse than thinking off, the remaining ~10 hours would not change the decision.

## Notes

- `lms load google/gemma-4-12b -c 16384 --parallel 1` reported a 262,144 context in `lms ps` for the
  MLX 12B build; the same flag gave 16,384 for e4b. Larger than requested, so no "context exceeded"
  failures, but the README's flag may not be honoured for every build.
